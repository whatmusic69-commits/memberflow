# Platform assets

Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest: original Simple Icons 13.21.0 SVGs (CC0-1.0).
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/13.21.0/icons

Wallet product logos retain their original colours and proportions:
- Apple Wallet, credited to Apple: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Apple_Wallet_Icon.svg
- Google Wallet, credited to Google: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_Wallet_icon.svg
Both source pages mark these geometric images as public domain. Brand names and logos remain their owners' trademarks.

Wallet logos accompany the respective product names in an illustrative product preview.
No functional Add to Wallet badges or live integration are presented.
Assets are served locally. General interface icons remain Lucide.

Colour variants:
- Instagram: unchanged Simple Icons glyph, with a local Instagram palette gradient.
- Pinterest: unchanged Simple Icons glyph, with brand red (#E60023).
- TikTok: original three-layer colour geometry from SVGL (MIT), with an explicit
  viewBox added to preserve proportions when scaled.
  Source: https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/c6660504a40721b89149d6bf77860c95715e174d/static/library/tiktok.svg
