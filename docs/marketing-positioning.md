# Marketing positioning

MemberFlow helps small businesses turn first-time visitors into repeat customers.
QR registration, personal customer cards, loyalty and recorded visit history lead
the story. Social distribution is a supplementary, planned capability.

## Copy changes

| Surface | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home H1 | Create once. Grow everywhere. | Turn one-time customers into regulars. |
| Home primary CTA | Start free | Create your loyalty programme |
| Benefits | Less manual work; Stay connected | More than a paper stamp card: personal QR, visible progress, recorded visits, dashboard management |
| Product overview | One experience. Both sides of your business. | Their QR card. Your visit history. |
| Product journey | One QR. Everything connected. | How do customers join through QR? |
| Product CRM | Know your customers. | See who visits again — and who has stopped. |
| Product offers | Turn visits into relationships. | Who needs a reason to return? |
| Solutions | Made for businesses like yours. | Give your customers a reason to return. |
| Pricing | A plan for your next stage. | Choose a plan for your repeat customers. |

All changed copy is supplied in EN/LV/RU. Navigation keeps its compact existing
CTA; the primary content CTAs describe setting up loyalty and retain the existing
onboarding destination. The shorter Home structure, components and motion remain.
No new marketing sections were added. Minor wrapping styles accommodate longer
CTA and channel labels.

Home metadata now uses the retention positioning. Product has a dedicated SEO
title and description. Solutions and Pricing metadata consume their updated copy
through existing metadata functions, preserving canonical and language alternates.

## Product claims

- Social publishing is explicitly **planned**, including on the existing Home
  campaign diagram and campaign preview. The workflow explains text + image,
  channel selection, supported formats, official APIs and platform approvals.
- Formats, permissions and approval requirements differ by platform. No claim
  that all networks support the same post or are already connected.
- Wallet is a convenient place for the personal loyalty card, where available.
  Live issuance requires the integration. Pass updates and notifications remain
  distinct; notification delivery is not guaranteed.
- Customer segmentation uses recorded visits. No revenue, ROI or conversion
  guarantees were added. Pricing configuration, limits and entitlements are unchanged.

## Verification limitation

No browser, tests, lint, typecheck or production build were run, respecting the
user's standing prohibition on checks. Responsiveness and the five-second message
test have not been verified in a browser. Existing server was not started or restarted.
